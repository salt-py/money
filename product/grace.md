# Grace

**Core need:** Understand long-term trends, not just this month.

Grace is less interested in this month's numbers in isolation and more
in the shape of her finances over years — is spending creeping up
gradually in a way no single month would reveal, does a particular
category reliably spike every December, is net worth actually compounding
the way it should be. She wants the tool to notice slow trends she
wouldn't catch by eyeballing one report at a time.

## Useful features

- ✅ `balances` snapshots per import + `report net-worth --since` — the
  underlying data already supports a trend view, even if today's reports
  are mostly single-period.
- 🔲 Year-over-year category comparison (this December vs. last
  December), not just month-over-month.
- 🔲 A genuine multi-month/multi-year trend view — a table or simple
  chart across many periods, not a point-in-time or single-range report.
- 🔲 A savings-rate-over-time view — income minus spend, tracked as a
  trend across months/years rather than reported for one month at a time.
