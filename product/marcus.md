# Marcus

**Core need:** See all his money in one place, with minimal effort.

Marcus's accounts are scattered across Monzo, a NatWest current account,
and a couple of credit cards. He isn't trying to optimize anything or
follow a strict budget — he just wants a single trustworthy number for
"where do I actually stand" without logging into four different apps and
adding it up in his head. What he values most is that the number is
complete (every account represented) and current (not quietly stale
because he forgot to re-import one account for a month).

## Useful features

- ✅ `report net-worth` — total and per-account balance in one place.
- ✅ Monzo API import + multi-bank PDF import — every account actually
  represented, not just the one with an API.
- ✅ `report net-worth --since` — a trend, not just a single snapshot.
- 🔲 A single "checkup" summary bundling net worth, this month's spend,
  and any Uncategorized backlog into one glance — see Ben, who wants the
  same thing for a different reason.
- 🔲 Stale-data warnings — flag an account that hasn't been re-imported
  in a while, so the "one number" doesn't quietly go out of date without
  Marcus noticing.
