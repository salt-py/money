# Elena

**Core need:** Track side income cleanly for tax time.

Elena has a day job plus freelance income on the side, and dreads the
annual scramble to reconstruct what she earned and spent on it from a
year of statements. What she wants is for that income and its associated
expenses to be organized as they happen — categorized distinctly from
her regular salary and day-to-day spend — rather than reconstructed
retroactively under deadline pressure.

## Useful features

- ✅ `Income` category + custom `config/rules.yaml` rules — side income
  can already be tagged distinctly from salary as it's imported.
- 🔲 A dedicated self-employed category group with tax-relevant
  sub-categories (equipment, software, mileage, and similar).
- 🔲 A report scoped to the UK tax year (6 Apr - 5 Apr), not just
  calendar months.
- 🔲 An export suitable for handing to an accountant or dropping into a
  self-assessment return, rather than reading figures off the terminal.
