# Priya

**Core need:** Stick to a realistic monthly budget.

Priya earns a decent income but has the familiar feeling that money
disappears every month without a clear sense of where. Generic budgeting
templates ("50/30/20") never quite fit her actual life, so she wants a
budget built from her *own* real spending history, not someone else's
rule of thumb. Just as important as getting the number right at the start
of the month is being able to check partway through whether she's on
track — finding out she's blown the Eating Out budget on the 28th isn't
useful.

## Useful features

- ✅ `money budget suggest` — a per-category budget proposed from trailing
  real spend, not a generic template.
- ✅ `money budget show` / `report budget --month` — actual vs. saved,
  with money in and out shown separately rather than netted.
- 🔲 Mid-month pacing — "you're 60% through the month and already at 80%
  of your Eating Out budget," not just an end-of-month verdict.
- 🔲 Rollover handling — a choice about whether unspent budget in one
  category carries into the next month or resets.
- 🔲 Budget history — seeing how the suggested/saved budget has shifted
  over time as habits actually change, not just the current snapshot.
