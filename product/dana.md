# Dana

**Core need:** Get out of debt, fastest and cheapest way possible.

Dana is carrying balances across a couple of credit cards and an
overdraft, each with a different APR and some with promotional periods
about to end. She's not looking for encouragement or generic tips — she
wants a straight answer to "if I throw an extra £150 at this every month,
when am I actually debt-free, and which balance should that money go to
first." Juggling several minimum payments and APRs in her head feels
error-prone and stressful; she wants the math done for her, honestly,
including the parts that are inconvenient (a promo rate ending, a
minimum payment that's barely covering interest).

## Useful features

- ✅ `money debt setup` / `show` / `plan` — avalanche payoff simulation
  (highest effective APR first) across every tracked debt.
- ✅ Promotional-rate support — the plan correctly re-targets once a 0%
  intro period ends, instead of assuming today's APR forever.
- ✅ `report discretionary` — an honest "what's actually free to put
  toward debt beyond the minimums" number, grounded in real spend.
- 🔲 Payoff milestones — a way to see (or be told) when a specific debt
  is fully cleared, or that a promo rate expires soon.
- 🔲 Side-by-side "what if" comparisons — e.g. £100/month extra vs.
  £200/month extra, shown together rather than as two separate runs.
- 🔲 Plan-vs-actual tracking over time — did this month's real extra
  payment keep pace with the plan, or has the debt-free date quietly
  slipped.
