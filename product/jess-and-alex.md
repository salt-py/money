# Jess & Alex

**Core need:** Split shared household costs fairly.

Jess and Alex mostly keep separate finances but funnel shared costs —
rent, bills, groceries — through a joint account, contributing an agreed
split each month (proportional to income, or 50/50). What they actually
want to know isn't just "money moved to the joint account" but whether
the *agreed split* is being honored over time, and by how much either of
them is over or under in a given month.

## Useful features

- ✅ `Household Contribution` category + `report household` — tracks the
  one-way transfer to a shared account and its share of income.
- 🔲 Two-sided reconciliation — compare both partners' contributions
  against an agreed split ratio, not just one person's outgoing transfer
  in isolation.
- 🔲 Shared-expense categories flagged distinctly from personal spend,
  even when paid from a personal account rather than the joint one.
- 🔲 A monthly "who owes who" summary, closing out any gap between the
  agreed split and what actually happened.
