# Sofia

**Core need:** Save toward a specific goal by a specific date.

Sofia isn't in debt and isn't struggling with day-to-day budgeting — she
has a specific target in mind (a house deposit, a wedding, a big trip)
and wants to know if she's actually on pace to hit it by when she needs
it, using real numbers rather than a hopeful guess. When she's not on
pace, she wants to know what would need to change (spend less where, or
by how much) rather than just being told "no."

## Useful features

- ✅ `report discretionary` — the honest "what's actually free to save"
  number any goal projection should be built on, rather than a naive
  income-minus-everything guess.
- 🔲 Named savings goals (target amount + target date), tracked alongside
  debts in their own config.
- 🔲 An "on pace" projection — at current discretionary income, will this
  goal be hit by its date, and if not, by how much is it short.
- 🔲 Multiple concurrent goals with priority ordering — a similar shape
  to the debt avalanche planner, but allocating savings instead of
  extra debt payments.
